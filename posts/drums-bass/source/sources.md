# Public research sources

Retrieved 2026-10-09 UTC. All are manufacturer manuals or manufacturer educational publications. Numerical examples marked C are independently calculated; listening moves marked W are editorial starting points, not measurements.

## S01 Ableton Live 12 Manual — Mixing
https://www.ableton.com/en/manual/mixing/
Section: 18.1.1 Additional Mixer Features
Scope: Floating-point internal headroom is not a license to clip physical I/O or fixed-point export; no universal kick peak target.

## S02 Shure — Microphone Techniques for Recording
https://www.shure.com/en-US/docs/education/Microphone-Techniques-for-Recording
Section: Phase; electrical output
Scope: Equal coherent sine sums; timing differences cause frequency-dependent phase relationships. Use definitions, not oversimplified broadband “180 degrees” phrasing.

## S03 FabFilter Pro-Q 4 Help — Spectrum analyzer
https://www.fabfilter.com/help/pro-q/using/analyzer
Section: Spectrum analyzer
Scope: Pre/Post/external display; collision suggestion is not proof of audible masking; analyzer resolution and tilt change display.

## S04 FabFilter Pro-Q 4 Help — Piano display
https://www.fabfilter.com/help/pro-q/using/pianodisplay
Section: Piano display
Scope: 88-key A0=27.5 Hz through C8; note labels and frequencies are related, not fixed tonal recipes.

## S05 FabFilter One Help — Filter
https://prod.fabfilter.com/help/one/using/filter
Section: Filter
Scope: Harmonics are multiples of fundamental in harmonic oscillator signals.

## S06 iZotope — How to Mix Kick and Bass
https://shopify.izotope.com/community/blog/how-to-mix-kick-and-bass
Section: Unmask overlapping frequencies; sidechain; choose notes
Scope: Arrangement, envelope length, tonal choices and conditional dynamic ducking. Do not reproduce article’s overly broad 90% or fixed kick-wins statements.

## S07 iZotope — Mastering low end: essential processing tips and tools
https://shopify.izotope.com/community/blog/mastering-low-end
Section: Harmonic support
Scope: Harmonics can preserve bass identity on restricted-bandwidth playback; not a universal 800–2500 Hz requirement.

## S08 iZotope — How to EQ Drums
https://www.izotope.com/community/blog/how-to-eq-drums
Section: Snare; toms and percussion; hi-hats
Scope: Timbre and arrangement determine EQ; dynamic EQ can tame intermittent harshness. Do not adopt mandatory HPF recipe from article.
