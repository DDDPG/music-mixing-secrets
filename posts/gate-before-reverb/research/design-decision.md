# Design decisions

The tutorial uses white #ffffff, pale gray #f5f5f7, restrained blue #0066cc and Noto Sans SC. Eight pages follow the topic: separate input/output routes, quantitative threshold example, genuine product reference, closing time diagram, range/selection distinction, listening checks, and a readable repository citation.

The actual FabFilter screenshot preserves its aspect ratio and colors. A crop teaches timing controls without inventing controls. Quantitative SVGs are author-created analytic examples, with state assumptions, units and limitations adjacent to their claims. Main content is readable at1080×1440 and checked at360px.
